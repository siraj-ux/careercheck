How Degree Courses Are Suggested to Students

Complete Technical & Psychometric Report Mapping Guide (Exclusively Focused on Courses & Specializations)
Domain: Academic Course Selection Engine Scope: 78 Database Courses Streams: Science (PCB/PCM), Commerce, Arts/Humanities Target: High School (11th/12th) & College Students
Core Principle: Course recommendation in CareerChecks is never random and never based on college tie-ups or commercial advertising. Instead, degree courses are suggested through a scientific Triple-Alignment Engine that cross-references a student's 12th Academic Stream Eligibility, their Psychometric Holland Code (RIASEC Interests), their Aptitude Strengths (Logical, Verbal, Spatial), and their Work Values.

1 The 5 Assessment Report Inputs Used for Course Matching

When a student completes their CareerChecks assessment, the system extracts 5 objective data points from their profile and answers. Every suggested degree course is mathematically derived from these 5 inputs:

1. Academic Stream (Hard Filter)
Identifies eligible disciplines from the Class 12 stage:
• Science PCB: Medical, Dental, AYUSH, Allied Health, Pharmacy, Life Sciences.
• Science PCM: Engineering (B.Tech), Architecture, Data Science, Physical Sciences.
• Commerce: B.Com, BBA, CA, ACCA, FinTech, Economics.
• Arts / Any Stream: BCA, Law, Design, Journalism, Psychology, Hotel Management.
2. Holland RIASEC Code (Interests)
Derived from the 60 Interests questions in Section 1:
• R Realistic: Hands-on, machines, physical work.
• I Investigative: Science, coding, math, research.
• A Artistic: Creativity, writing, UI/UX, media.
• S Social: Healthcare, teaching, counseling, society.
• E Enterprising: Business, leadership, management.
• C Conventional: Accounting, data, organization.
3. Aptitude & Reasoning Profile
Evaluates cognitive ability across 3 core dimensions:
• Logical (0-100%): Crucial for Engineering, Coding, Math, and FinTech.
• Verbal (0-100%): Crucial for Law, Journalism, Medicine, and Management.
• Spatial (0-100%): Crucial for Architecture, Design, Mechatronics, and Surgery.
4. Big-5 Personality Spectrums
Maps working style:
• Practical vs. Imaginative: Applied execution vs. conceptual design.
• Spontaneous vs. Organised: Dynamic creative roles vs. structured clinical/audit workflows.
• Reserved vs. Outgoing: Solo analytical work vs. public-facing leadership.
5. Core Career Values
Identifies what motivates the student:
• Altruism & Social Impact: Medicine, Nursing, Allied Health, Teaching.
• High Income & Prestige: B.Tech AI, MBBS, CA/ACCA, Corporate Law.
• Autonomy & Innovation: Startups, BBA, Data Science, Design.

2 The 4-Stage Course Recommendation Algorithm

The recommendation engine evaluates all 78 courses in the CareerChecks database through four sequential calculation gates:

GATE 1: Academic Stream & Subject Eligibility Filter Hard Rule
Eliminates courses that the student cannot legally or practically pursue based on their Class 12 board subjects. For example, a student from a pure Commerce or Arts background is never recommended B.Tech Mechanical or MBBS, preventing unrealistic or invalid aspirations.
GATE 2: RIASEC Vector Cosine Similarity Match Psychometric Affinity
The student's 6-dimensional interest score (R, I, A, S, E, C) is compared with the ideal benchmark profile of each course using vector cosine similarity. For instance:
• MBBS / BDS / Allied Health: Demands high I (Investigation/Biology) and high S (Social service/Patient empathy).
• B.Tech CSE / AI & ML: Demands high I (Logic/Coding) and high R (System hardware/building).
• BBA / B.Com with CA: Demands high E (Business/Persuasion) and high C (Accounts/Compliance).
GATE 3: Cognitive Aptitude Threshold Verification Capability Verification
Verifies that the student has the cognitive strengths required to complete the curriculum without burnout:
• If a course has heavy advanced mathematics (e.g., B.Tech Data Science, VLSI), Logical Aptitude must meet minimum benchmark.
• If a course has heavy spatial and 3D perception (e.g., B.Arch, Multimedia, Surgical Tech), Spatial Aptitude is verified.
• If a course relies on legal texts or complex communication (e.g., Journalism, BBA, Clinical Therapy), Verbal Aptitude is verified.
GATE 4: Categorization into Primary, Alternative & Backup Tracks Strategic Guidance
Courses passing Gates 1-3 are ranked and categorized into 3 strategic career tracks:
• Primary Degree Track (Best Fit: 90%+ Match): The highest aligned degree matching interest, aptitude, and aspirations.
• Alternative Degree Track (High Fit: 80-89% Match): A parallel specialized branch with strong employment overlap.
• Pragmatic Backup Track (Safe Pivot: 70-79% Match): A smart, lower-admission-risk course sharing the same foundational career path.
